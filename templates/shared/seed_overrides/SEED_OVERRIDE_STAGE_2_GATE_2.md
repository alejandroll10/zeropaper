### Seeded-mode override (applies because `seeded: true` in `pipeline_state.json`)

**Gate 2 negative verdict** (FAIL in theory-first; REVISE in empirical-first mechanism mode): revise the theory/mechanism **in place** while preserving what the seed pins. Do NOT regenerate a fundamentally different theory/mechanism, and do NOT invoke the sketch-swap / pivot authority the default routing offers — the seed pins this contribution. **This holds even when branch-manager returns RESTRUCTURE:** the every-3rd-version branch-manager check still fires as a useful diagnostic, but its RESTRUCTURE verdict cannot trigger a sketch-swap here — record the RESTRUCTURE reasoning in `process_log/pivot_log.md` and continue revising in place. **This in-place rule supersedes the base Gate-2 escalation, but not its first move.** The base path's *retire-the-class* step — cut the claims a recurring error class attaches to back to what was actually verified, across every claim of that shape — is the same move as the ship-honest check below and applies here, **restricted to claims that are `auxiliary` under the *Referent for "what the seed pins"* test below**. Retiring a class must never narrow a seed-pinned *core* claim: if the recurring class attaches to the core, prove the general version — and if the proof will not go through, keep revising in place rather than cutting. **This clause never fires the ship-honest check early.** A core-attached class is decided at the 3-failure cap below exactly like any other, consistent with the seeded principle that the burden is on establishing the *core itself* is dead before any halt — a recurrence at version 2 is not that establishment. (The base path carries no core/auxiliary distinction because outside seeded mode there is nothing pinned; here there is.) What is superseded is the base path's last resort: in theory-first, "the class survives being retired → increment `theory_attempt`, reset `theory_version`" would open a fresh-approach attempt — the "fundamentally different theory" this override forbids. In empirical-first, a surviving class instead escalates into the sketch-swap authority, which the opening paragraph already supersedes. **The base path's hard cap is also superseded — in its escape routes, not in its binding force.** That cap (3 consecutive math-audit failures in theory-first; 3 consecutive REVISEs in empirical-first) fires here exactly as it does on the base path, and "patch the current draft again" is no more available under seeding than it is there. What is unavailable is *both* of the escapes the base path offers at the cap, since this override forbids incrementing `theory_attempt` and forbids sketch-swap. So under `seeded: true` the cap routes into the **ship-honest check below**, which fires at the identical 3-consecutive-failure threshold and is the seeded equivalent escalation — narrow or negative-report an auxiliary claim, or halt with `abandon_report.md` if nothing ships. Reaching the cap and continuing to patch is not a legal seeded move either.

Either way, under `seeded: true` a surviving class does not open a new attempt or a swap: keep revising in place until the cap, then take the ship-honest branch. The 3-failure count below is keyed to **consecutive Gate-2 negative verdicts**, not to class-survival judgments — it advances on every consecutive FAIL/REVISE regardless of how the recurrence question was decided, so the ship-honest check can never be deferred by judging each recurrence to be a new class.

After 3 consecutive failures on the same theory/mechanism, do **not** immediately halt. First apply the **ship-honest check**: is there a reachable partial, narrowed, or negative result that still honors what the seed pins?

*Referent for "what the seed pins" (the core the check protects):* under `--faithful`, the load-bearing claims are enumerated in `output/seed/mechanism_contract.md` (its **Stated contribution** and **Theorem-statement constraints** sections) — a failure is *core* iff it breaks one of those, else it is *auxiliary*. Under plain `--seed` (no contract is extracted), the referent is the seed's stated central result as captured in `output/seed/` and `output/stage1/selected_idea.md`; when the core/auxiliary line is genuinely unclear from those, treat the failed item as **auxiliary and narrow it** (the seeded core principle is *never silently abandon the seed*, so the burden is on establishing that the *core itself* is dead before any halt).

Concretely: is the failing item an auxiliary implication/corollary that is over-broad, or a result that holds only under a documented restriction, while the seed's central result still stands? **If yes** → document the failed/blocked piece in `output/seed/limitations.md`, then delete the overclaim, restrict its scope, or record it as an honest negative — applying that to *every* claim of the same shape in the draft, not only the instance the auditor flagged (a narrowing that repairs the flagged instance and leaves its siblings standing is exactly why the class recurs) — and continue the pipeline with what ships. This is the faithful ship-honestly doctrine wired to this gate, **not** a pivot: the seed's pinned contribution is unchanged, only an auxiliary/presentation claim is narrowed. **Only if nothing shippable remains** — the seed's central result itself cannot be established and no honest partial survives — → stop and write `output/seed/abandon_report.md` with a post-mortem. Either way, do not silently increment `theory_attempt` into a new idea.
