### Seeded-mode override (applies because `seeded: true` in `pipeline_state.json`)

**Gate 2 negative verdict** (FAIL in theory-first; REVISE in empirical-first mechanism mode): revise the theory/mechanism **in place** while preserving what the seed pins. Do NOT regenerate a fundamentally different theory/mechanism, and do NOT invoke the sketch-swap / pivot authority the default routing offers — the seed pins this contribution. **This holds even when branch-manager returns RESTRUCTURE:** the every-3rd-version branch-manager check still fires as a useful diagnostic, but its RESTRUCTURE verdict cannot trigger a sketch-swap here — record the RESTRUCTURE reasoning in `process_log/pivot_log.md` and continue revising in place. **This in-place rule also supersedes the base Gate-2 plateau escalation.** In theory-first, the base path "errors plateau or increase across two consecutive attempts → increment `theory_attempt`, reset `theory_version`" would open a fresh-approach attempt — the "fundamentally different theory" this override forbids. In empirical-first, a plateau instead escalates into the sketch-swap authority, which the opening paragraph already supersedes. Either way, under `seeded: true` a plateau does not open a new attempt or a swap: keep revising in place and fold it into the 3-failure count below.

After 3 consecutive failures on the same theory/mechanism, do **not** immediately halt. First apply the **ship-honest check**: is there a reachable partial, narrowed, or negative result that still honors what the seed pins?

*Referent for "what the seed pins" (the core the check protects):* under `--faithful`, the load-bearing claims are enumerated in `output/seed/mechanism_contract.md` (its **Stated contribution** and **Theorem-statement constraints** sections) — a failure is *core* iff it breaks one of those, else it is *auxiliary*. Under plain `--seed` (no contract is extracted), the referent is the seed's stated central result as captured in `output/seed/` and `output/stage1/selected_idea.md`; when the core/auxiliary line is genuinely unclear from those, treat the failed item as **auxiliary and narrow it** (the seeded core principle is *never silently abandon the seed*, so the burden is on establishing that the *core itself* is dead before any halt).

Concretely: is the failing item an auxiliary implication/corollary that is over-broad, or a result that holds only under a documented restriction, while the seed's central result still stands? **If yes** → document the failed/blocked piece in `output/seed/limitations.md`, then delete the overclaim, restrict its scope, or record it as an honest negative, and continue the pipeline with what ships. This is the faithful ship-honestly doctrine wired to this gate, **not** a pivot: the seed's pinned contribution is unchanged, only an auxiliary/presentation claim is narrowed. **Only if nothing shippable remains** — the seed's central result itself cannot be established and no honest partial survives — → stop and write `output/seed/abandon_report.md` with a post-mortem. Either way, do not silently increment `theory_attempt` into a new idea.
