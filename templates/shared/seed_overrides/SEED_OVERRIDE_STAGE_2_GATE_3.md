### Seeded-mode override (applies because `seeded: true` in `pipeline_state.json`)

**Gate 3 KNOWN**: report the concern to `output/seed/novelty_concern_theory.md`. Try **one reformulation** that preserves what the seed pins (different notation, added heterogeneity, different equilibrium selection — anything that might surface a genuinely novel angle without changing what the theory is about). If still KNOWN after one reformulation, stop and write `output/seed/abandon_report.md`. Do not abandon for a different theory. (KNOWN is a directly-prior result; seed — unlike faithful — is permitted to pivot to a different idea when the result already exists in the literature.)

**Gate 3 INCREMENTAL**: do **not** abandon and do **not** hard-stop here. Report the concern to `output/seed/novelty_concern_theory.md` for honest positioning, then **proceed to Stage 4**. The scorer applies its own H4 distinguishing-result test (INCREMENTAL with a distinguishing result — a new comparative static, sign reversal, extra assumption that changes the conclusion, or new empirical implication — passes H4 and is scored on merits; only INCREMENTAL with *no* distinguishing result fails). If H4 fails at Gate 4, the scorer's ABANDON path handles it there with branch-manager and the full escalation ladder — not a bare Gate-3 hard stop on a math-valid theory. This mirrors faithful mode's document-and-proceed treatment of the same signal.

> **Known limitation (shared with faithful mode):** proceeding straight to Stage 4 here skips Stage 2b (exploration) and Stage 3 (implications), which normally run only after a NOVEL verdict. So the calibration check is foregone and `implications.md` is absent — the scorer's Surprise cap/floor rules (gated on `implications.md` existing) will not fire for a seed-INCREMENTAL theory. The scorer tolerates the missing file; this is an accepted trade to reach scoring cheaply without re-running expensive exploration on a pinned seed.
