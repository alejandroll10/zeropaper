### Seeded-mode override (applies because `seeded: true` in `pipeline_state.json`)

**Gate 1c BLOCKED** (either `BLOCKED-DIFFICULTY` or `BLOCKED-IMPOSSIBLE`): do NOT abandon the idea. Report the blockage to `output/seed/prototype_blockage.md`, then try **one alternative formalization** (different state variable, different equilibrium concept, different time structure — anything that preserves the seed's mechanism). If the alternative is still BLOCKED, stop and append the post-mortem to `output/seed/prototype_blockage.md`; do not swap the idea.

This seeded re-formalization **supersedes** the non-seeded Gate 1c machinery: the NOVEL-gated harder-technique retry (Gate 1c item 3) and the portfolio guard (Step 2a) are both skipped under `seeded: true` — the seed is the contract, so a blocked seed is re-formalized in place, never triaged against a portfolio of alternative ideas. (A `BLOCKED-IMPOSSIBLE` final verdict still propagates its Negative result per Gate 1c item 4; a `BLOCKED-DIFFICULTY` does not.)
