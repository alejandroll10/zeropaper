## Outlet notes

**AER Insights** — AEA-published short-format sister journal to *American Economic Review*, launched 2019. Placed in the **top-5** tier on a quality-bar read: the AEA holds AER Insights to the "same standards" as AER (≤6k words, ~4.7% acceptance), so a paper must clear an AER-level bar to get in — it is the short-format route into top-5, not a lower-quality one. **Quality bar vs. CV credit:** the market read still lags the editorial bar — IDEAS 10-year rank ~21 (sitting with JME and JEEA), WoS JIF ~6.7–8.1, tenure treatment heterogeneous (near-top-5 at the elite institutions whose faculty sit on the editorial board — MIT, Harvard, Stanford, Yale, Princeton — counted-as-field at most other R1s). The tier placement here reflects the **quality bar the paper must clear**, not the CV weight. Format constraints: ≤6,000 words, fast turnaround, "same standards" as AER per AEA. Within top-5, it is the best-fit outlet for a paper that is tight, single-mechanism, and already fits these caps as written; longer multi-mechanism papers that clear the top-5 bar go to AER/Econometrica/QJE/JPE/ReStud, not AER Insights.
