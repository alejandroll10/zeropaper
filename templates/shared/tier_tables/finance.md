## Outlet notes

**JF Insights & Perspectives (JFIP)** — AFA-published sister journal to *Journal of Finance*, submission portal opened January 2026. Placed in the **top-3-fin** tier on a quality-bar read: the AFA frames JFIP as JF-equivalent, so a paper must clear a JF-level bar to get in — it is simply the short-format route, not a lower-quality one. **Quality bar vs. CV credit:** the market has not yet absorbed the JF-equivalent framing (IDEAS / EJMR signal: "above JFQA, tenure-counts-as-field at most institutions through the late 2020s"), so CV/tenure credit currently lags the editorial bar. The tier placement here reflects the **quality bar the paper must clear**, not the CV weight — do not read top-3-fin placement as a claim about tenure credit. Format constraints: ≤7,000 words main text, ≤5 exhibits (each reduces budget by 200 words), single-insight, in-or-out review (no R&R). Within top-3-fin, it is the best-fit outlet for a paper that is tight, single-mechanism, and already fits these caps as written; sprawling multi-mechanism papers that clear the top-3 bar go to JF/JFE/RFS, not JFIP.
