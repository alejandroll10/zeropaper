## Outlet notes

**Nature-family venues** — the `nature` tier is aspirational, not the default target: it fits a landmark finding with resonance far beyond ML (e.g., a capacity law for machine cognition that speaks to the human working-memory literature), written for a general scientific reader. The bar is Nature/Science-level or top-ML best-paper-level work — a paper that would merely place at a Nature-family specialty journal is a `top-ml` paper, not a `nature` paper. Most strong papers in this domain belong at `top-ml`, and a paper is not downgraded for targeting `top-ml` from the start.

**TMLR** — rolling-submission journal whose editorial bar is *correctness and clarity of claims*, explicitly not perceived significance. Placed in the **field** tier on the venue's own framing, but note the fit asymmetry: a careful measurement paper whose claimed significance is modest is often *better served* by TMLR than by a borderline top-ml submission, because the review criteria reward exactly what this pipeline's audit chain enforces (claims supported by evidence, uncertainty quantified, no over-claiming). A downgrade from `top-ml` to `field` should route here by default.

**Conference cadence** — NeurIPS/ICML/ICLR run on fixed annual deadlines with no rolling submission and no R&R (one rebuttal round). The pipeline's revision loops model the rebuttal + resubmit-next-cycle reality: a Reject-and-resubmit at the same tier is a normal outcome, not an escalation trigger by itself.
