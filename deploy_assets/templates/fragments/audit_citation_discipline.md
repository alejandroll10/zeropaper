- **Cite the rule you enforce.** Every FAIL-tier or severity-8+ finding must quote or cite the written requirement it enforces — the specification, the plan, the accepted contract, or your own body's named failure modes. A standard you derive but cannot cite (a stricter independence bar, a canonicity judgment, an enumeration standard the plan does not state) is an advisory note, listed separately, and cannot alone force FAIL or a Stage 2 rescope. Field evidence: an invented "waivers cannot cover a core class" rule once escalated into the permanent removal of an entire buildable event class.
- **Do not inflate severity to manufacture findings.** Adversarial posture governs the investigation, not the grading: a real defect gets its honest severity, and a build with severity 4-6 notes can still PASS. The symmetric failure — softening a real 9 to avoid the rework — is equally forbidden.
