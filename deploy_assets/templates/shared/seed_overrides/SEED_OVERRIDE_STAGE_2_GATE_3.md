### Seeded-mode override (applies because `seeded: true` in `pipeline_state.json`)

**Gate 3 KNOWN**: report the concern to `output/seed/novelty_concern_theory.md` (`theory` in this legacy filename means the current Stage 2 artifact in every mode). Try **one reformulation** that preserves what the seed pins — a different formulation, scope boundary, dimension, or other alteration that might surface a genuinely novel angle without changing what the Stage 2 contribution is about. If still KNOWN after one reformulation, stop and write `output/seed/abandon_report.md`. Do not abandon for a different theory. (KNOWN is a directly-prior result; seed — unlike faithful — is permitted to pivot to a different idea when the result already exists in the literature.)

**Gate 3 INCREMENTAL**: do **not** abandon and do **not** hard-stop here. This route is for a genuine INCREMENTAL — the report names the distinguishing result the work still carries. A report that names none, or whose `## Trajectory` section says the elements an earlier verdict rested on are gone and nothing replaced them, is a KNOWN in all but its label: take the KNOWN route above rather than proceeding on a verdict the checker's own text does not support. Otherwise, report the concern to `output/seed/novelty_concern_theory.md` for honest positioning (name what carries the contribution now and what earlier verdicts rested on, so the narrowing is visible in one place), then **run Stage 3 (implications) and follow the normal route through every applicable Stage 3a/3b evidence stage and audit before Gate 4**. **Stage 2b remains omitted; this routing does not waive any mode- or extension-specific replacement evidence, deferred audit, or Gate 4 entry check.** Stage 3 is not optional: `output/stage3/implications.md` is a hard input to the scorer's Surprise cap/floor rules at Gate 4, to `paper-writer` at Stage 5, and to `puzzle-triager`. The scorer still records its H4 judgment, but under the seeded Gate 4 route that judgment is diagnostic rather than a trigger for branch-manager or abandonment. This mirrors faithful mode's document-and-proceed treatment of the same signal.

> **Known limitation (shared with faithful mode):** on the modeless theory-first route, this still forgoes Stage 2b numerical exploration on the pinned result before scoring. No other stage or gate is waived.
