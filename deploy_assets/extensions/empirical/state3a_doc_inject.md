**`stage2_mechanism_version`** (consumed only under `--mode empirical-first`)**:** Set to the `theory_version` for which the Stage 2 Gate 2 **mechanism-plausibility** audit (`mechanism-auditor`) last returned PLAUSIBLE. Before advancing at Gate 4, the orchestrator must verify this equals the current `theory_version`; if it is stale, re-run the Stage 2 Gate 2 audit on the revised mechanism before proceeding (see `docs/stage_2.md` "Gate 2: Mechanism Plausibility" and `docs/stage_3a_empirical.md` "Re-fire on theory revision" — both sections render only under `--mode empirical-first`). This is the empirical-first analogue of the theory-first `stage2b_theory_version` Gate-4 block and sits parallel to `stage3a_theory_version`: a downstream mutate (Gate 4 REVISE → Stage 2, Stage 6 Major Revision → theory-generator) must re-pass the mechanism gate, not just re-run the empirics. **Outside `--mode empirical-first`** (a theory-first `--ext empirical` run) this field is initialized to `null` and never updated — `mechanism-auditor` is pruned and Gate 2 is the math audit — so the staleness rule that consumes it does not fire; it is the mirror of `stage2b_theory_version`'s null/no-op status under empirical-first.
