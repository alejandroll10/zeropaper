NOVEL (Gate 1b)

The idea that imperfect deposit-flow control makes deposit inflows reduce shareholder value (negative deposit marginal q) under leverage regulation is new relative to the build-on priors: Drechsler-Savov-Schnabl (2017, 2021) treat deposits as a valuable cheap/sticky liability, and Leland (1994a) / Brunnermeier-Sannikov (2014) model dynamic capital structure without a stochastic deposit-flow state variable.
