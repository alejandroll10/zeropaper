NOVEL (Gate 1b)

The idea — that delegating contracting to the more skilled agent restores commitment under bilateral private team contracts via an observability effect — is new relative to the builds-on priors (Holmstrom 1982 first-best under public team contracts; Segal 1999 private-offer deviation; Aghion and Tirole 1997 single-agent delegation under double-sided moral hazard).
