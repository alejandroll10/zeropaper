NOVEL (Gate 1b). Randomizing financial education to top executives of medium and large firms supplies the first experimental test of the expertise-to-policy link that prior work (Bertrand and Schoar (2003); Custódio and Metzger (2014)) established only non-experimentally, and extends the training-RCT literature (Bloom et al. (2013); Bruhn and Zia (2013)) from microentrepreneurs/operational practices to top executives and financial practices.
