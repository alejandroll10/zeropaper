# Novelty check — idea (Gate 1b)

**NOVEL.** Identifying the loan officer's own race as the supply-side source of the residual minority mortgage gap — and separating information advantage from taste via joint approval-and-default tests — goes beyond the priors (Munnell et al. 1996; Bhutta, Hizmo & Ringo 2024; Fisman, Paravisini & Vig 2017; Ambrose, Conklin & Lopez 2021; Bartlett et al. 2022), which document the gap or borrower/broker-race effects but not the loan-officer-race channel.
