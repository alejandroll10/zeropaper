# Novelty check — idea (Gate 1b)

**Verdict: NOVEL**

Constructing pricing factors from individual investor holdings via a spanning
condition (Investor Pricing Factors) is new relative to the relatesTo priors,
which it only builds on for theory (Merton 1973), motivation (Balasubramaniam et
al. 2023), and methodology (Fama-French 2018, Kozak-Nagel-Santosh 2020) or
extends (Betermier-Calvet-Sodini 2017).
