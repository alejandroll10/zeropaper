# Novelty check — idea (Gate 1b)

**Verdict: NOVEL**

Distinguishing the two dimensions of the big data revolution — lower processing cost $$c$$ and a larger data frontier $$\tau_{dm}^{\text{max}}$$ — and showing they can have opposite effects on quant search intensity and capital allocation is not established by the priors it builds on (Garleanu and Pedersen 2018; Grossman and Stiglitz 1980; Verrecchia 1982; Han and Sangiorgi 2018; Banerjee and Breon-Drish 2021).
