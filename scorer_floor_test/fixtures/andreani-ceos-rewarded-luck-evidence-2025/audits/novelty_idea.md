# Novelty audit — idea (Gate 1b)

**NOVEL.** Using an exogenous, one-off corporate-tax-rate cut as a clean luck shock to test asymmetric, scrutiny-conditioned pay-for-luck is new relative to the priors it engages (Bertrand and Mullainathan 2001; Garvey and Milbourn 2006; Daniel, Li, and Naveen 2020; Blanchard, Lopez-de-Silanes, and Shleifer 1994).
