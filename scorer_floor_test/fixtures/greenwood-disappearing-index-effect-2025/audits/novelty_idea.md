# Novelty check — idea (Gate 1b)

**Verdict: NOVEL**

The economic decomposition of the index-effect decline into a migration (offsetting-demand) channel and a falling demand-curve multiplier $M$ goes beyond the priors: Shleifer (1986) and Harris and Gurel (1986) documented the effect's existence, and Bennett, Stulz, and Wang (2020) / Preston and Soe (2021) noted its decline, but none provide this demand-shock-versus-multiplier attribution.
